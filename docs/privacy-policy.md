# First Spark Privacy Policy

#### Date of Last Revision: March 03, 2025

## 1. About this document

First Spark's Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share the personal information
you decide to share and will help you make informed decisions when accessing or using the First
Spark website, located at [www.firstspark.global](https://firstspark.global/), or any successor
websites, including any and all international and mobile versions thereof (collectively, the
"Website").

- By registering with First Spark, using or accessing the Website, you are agreeing to the practices
  described in this Privacy Policy. Your use of the Website is also subject to the First Spark Code
  of Conduct and Terms of Use. If you do not agree with these policies, do not access or use our
  Services or interact with any other aspect of our business.

- If you have questions or concerns regarding this statement, you should contact our staff at
  support@firstspark.global.

## 2. First Spark's Privacy Principles

First Spark operates as a non-profit, social impact organization. We are powered by volunteer
mentors who care deeply, a community based on mutual trust, and the belief that a better world is
possible.

* We will never sell or rent your data to third parties.
* Your profile information is public.
* Other information that you provide to First Spark is confidential.
* Your conversations with others in the First Spark community are confidential. It is also your
  responsibility to help us keep it that way.
* General information about your activity on the site helps us to do our job.
* We only share data with trusted partners in order to: improve our service, to measure our impact,
  and identify misuse, and to respond to your requests.
* If you join First Spark through your employer or other organization, some information about your
  activity will be shared with them.
* You can control how we communicate with you.
* You have rights to the information that we request or have collected about you.
* You can ask to be removed from the community.
* We are based in the United States, and data is transferred and held securely within US-based data
  centers. When our global operations require us to transfer information to other countries we
  utilize appropriate safeguards.
* You can contact us at any time at support@firstspark.global.
